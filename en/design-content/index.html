02 / Working together

From briefing to usable asset.

The process creates clarity before design goes into production. Feedback and handover are tailored to the agreed scope.

Workflow01 → 04
  1. Clarify the objectives and framework: Agree on the audience, brand materials, channels, formats and responsibilities.
  2. Develop the concept: Define the core message and visual direction for the agreed content.
  3. Develop the designs: Create assets, consolidate feedback and implement agreed changes.
  4. Hand over clearly: Provide final files and agreed variations in usable formats.

Scope, feedback rounds and timing are agreed for each project.

03 / Design in context

The right format for every channel.

A piece of content cannot always be used unchanged everywhere. The message stays consistent; its structure, length and visual emphasis reflect the specific touchpoint.

  • Website: clear hierarchy and visual guidance for key messages.
  • Social Media: concise, recognisable content suited to the agreed channel.
  • Video and motion: movement and editing that make a specific message easy to understand.
  • Presentation: clear visuals for conversations, pitches or internal communication.

Typical example · illustrative scenario

A B2B provider needs a consistent presence for a new solution.

Scenario
The website, social posts and sales presentation currently explain the same solution using different messages and visual styles.
Approach
Agree the core message and existing brand rules, develop a visual set from them and produce content tailored to each channel.
Outcome
An agreed set of consistent, channel-specific files that the team can use on the website, on social media and in conversations.

Note: This is an example process, not a reference or a claim about a specific client's results.

04 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions about B2B design & content.

Specific answers about scope, collaboration and applications.

Which design and content services does Bayram Dökmeci offer?

Bayram Dökmeci supports B2B businesses and agencies with visual design, conversion-focused web assets, brand updates, motion design and cross-channel content planning. The scope is agreed to suit the audience, channels and existing brand guidelines.

Who is this service suitable for?

The service is exclusively for B2B businesses and agencies in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. It suits organisations looking to create consistent digital content or visual brand elements while working directly with a German-speaking contact.

How does a design or content project work?

We start by agreeing the audience, communication objective, channels and existing brand materials. This is followed by concepts and designs, feedback rounds and production of the agreed assets in suitable formats. Scope and timing depend on the project.

What content can be created for social media and video?

Examples include visual social media elements, short motion graphics, video edits and variations for agreed digital channels. Topics, format, source material and distribution are agreed together before production.

Does Bayram Dökmeci also work with agencies?

Yes. Agencies in DACH can engage Bayram Dökmeci directly for project-based design and content work. Behind-the-scenes or white-label collaboration can be arranged by agreement.
