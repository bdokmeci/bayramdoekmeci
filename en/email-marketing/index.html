03 / Know what moves things forward

Not just opened.
Making a difference.

I align evaluation with your objective: who clicks on relevant content? Who makes an enquiry? Which journey leads to a meaningful next step?

A transparent example calculation: 120 contacts clicking from 1,000 delivered emails gives a 12% click rate. If this produces 12 enquiries, that represents 10% of clicking contacts and 1.2% of delivered emails.

Deliverability, bounces and unsubscribes also matter. SPF, DKIM, DMARC, list maintenance and sender reputation form part of the technical review of your setup.

Answers to the key questions about working together.

Ask me directly
Who is your email marketing suitable for?

I support business clients and agencies exclusively in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. This service suits businesses looking to build or improve newsletters, lead nurturing or automated email journeys.

Can you take over my existing email tool?

Yes. We first review your existing tool, list structure, templates and integrations. Whether we continue with the current system or a change makes sense depends on your requirements. If needed, migration is planned as a separate scope of work.

How do you handle consent and data protection?

The technical setup accounts for consent status, double opt-in, unsubscribe options and agreed tracking settings. Before starting, we also clarify access, responsibilities and requirements for working together from Türkiye. We coordinate the legal assessment and approval with your data protection or legal contact.

What results can you promise?

I do not promise fixed open rates, revenue or lead numbers. We define suitable objectives and review factors including delivery, clicks, enquiries and unsubscribes. The figures on this page are purely illustrative calculations.

Do you also support agencies behind the scenes?

Yes. I support agencies with concepts, templates, automation and campaign delivery. White-label collaboration, communication channels and approvals are agreed to suit the project. You have a direct German-speaking contact.

What does working together cost, and how do we start?

Costs depend on the condition of your setup, the content required and the scope of automation. During a no-obligation initial consultation, we clarify objectives and your starting point. You then receive an agreed scope, proposal and timeline before implementation begins.
