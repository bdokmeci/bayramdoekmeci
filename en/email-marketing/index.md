## Not just opened. Making a difference.

I align evaluation with your objective: who clicks on relevant content? Who makes an enquiry? Which journey leads to a meaningful next step?

**A transparent example calculation:** 120 contacts clicking from 1,000 delivered emails gives a 12% click rate. If this produces 12 enquiries, that represents 10% of clicking contacts and 1.2% of delivered emails.

Deliverability, bounces and unsubscribes also matter. SPF, DKIM, DMARC, list maintenance and sender reputation form part of the technical review of your setup.
