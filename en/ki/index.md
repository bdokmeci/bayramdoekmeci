What is AI automation for businesses?

AI automation connects the processing of text or knowledge with defined process rules and existing systems. Bayram Dökmeci from MeinB2B develops AI workflows for this purpose, such as structuring B2B enquiries, drafting responses and controlled handover to the CRM. The service is exclusively for companies and agencies in Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

Which processes suit a first AI workflow?

A good starting point is a recurring, clearly defined process with available data and verifiable results. Typical examples include categorising enquiries, extracting information and preparing responses. In the initial consultation, we clarify whether AI makes a useful contribution or whether a fixed automation rule is sufficient.

Can you connect our existing tools?

Yes, integrating existing CRM, marketing and knowledge systems can be part of the scope. We first review available interfaces, permissions and the quality of the source data. We decide which systems and AI models will actually be used after that review.

What happens with sensitive data and incorrect answers?

Before implementation, we agree which data may be processed, who gets access and which provider is used. AI results can be incorrect or incomplete, so we plan validation rules, clarification routes and human approvals for relevant steps. Storage and deletion requirements are defined for each project.

Cost and duration depend on data quality, integrations, approvals and the workflow's scope. After initial process discovery, you receive a defined proposal with scope, timetable and quote. Ongoing model, tool and support costs are considered separately.
