This website is purely informational. I do not use analytics or tracking tools, advertising pixels or forms, and I do not load fonts from third-party servers. A JavaScript library is loaded via a content delivery network to display certain pages (see section 4). I do not set any cookies myself.

When you access the website, Cloudflare processes technically necessary connection data (including IP address, date and time, the page accessed, and browser and system information) in server logs to deliver the website, maintain security and prevent attacks. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure and efficient operation). Further information: Cloudflare privacy policy.

4. Alpine.js via the jsDelivr CDN

On certain pages, the Alpine.js JavaScript library is loaded via the jsDelivr content delivery network (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Your browser connects to jsDelivr servers, and technically necessary data (including your IP address, the time of access and browser information) is transmitted. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in delivering an efficient and maintainable website). Further information: jsDelivr privacy policy.

5. Contacting me

If you contact me by email, phone or WhatsApp, I process your details to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. I delete the data once it is no longer necessary for its purpose and no statutory retention obligations prevent deletion. If you use WhatsApp, the provider's privacy policy also applies.

6. External links

When you click external links (for example, to WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Calendly or GitHub), you leave this website. The respective provider is responsible for data processing there. If you book an appointment through Calendly, Calendly processes the data you enter (for example, your name, email address and preferred appointment), including in the USA. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Calendly's privacy policy applies.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection to processing (Articles 15 to 21 GDPR). Please use the contact details above to exercise these rights. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
