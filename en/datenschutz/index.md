Further information: [Cloudflare privacy policy](<https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/>).

## 4. Alpine.js via the jsDelivr CDN

On certain pages, the Alpine.js JavaScript library is loaded via the jsDelivr content delivery network (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Your browser connects to jsDelivr servers, and technically necessary data (including your IP address, the time of access and browser information) is transmitted. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in delivering an efficient and maintainable website). Further information: [jsDelivr privacy policy](<https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net>).
