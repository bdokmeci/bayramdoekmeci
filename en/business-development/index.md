Which B2B businesses is LinkedIn suitable for?

LinkedIn is an option when relevant contacts can be reached there and your offering addresses a specific business need. Typical use cases include software solutions that need explaining, consulting services or agency offerings. Before starting, we review your audience, reasons for conversation and the channel’s suitability for your plans.

What is automated in marketing automation?

Agreed internal tasks such as lead assignment, CRM updates, reminders and data handovers through approved interfaces are automated. Personal LinkedIn outreach and the assessment of a conversation remain human tasks. We determine which workflows make sense based on your systems and available data.

Here, a lead is considered qualified when target customer fit, a relevant contact role, specific need and next step have been clarified. We agree these criteria with your sales team before starting. We assess suitable dialogues, qualified conversations and their subsequent development; a particular number of leads or closed deals is not guaranteed.
